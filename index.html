Pehrav1.0.1

Your IP cameras and NVR recordings, on the big screen. A free Apple TV app, live on the App Store.

Pehra connects directly to cameras on your local network, with no account and no cloud subscription. It is built in Kotlin with Compose Multiplatform, running on tvOS through my own Compose Multiplatform for tvOS port, with video playback via libVLC.

  • Live view and a Multi View grid
  • Snapshots and recording playback
  • RTSP / ONVIF, Dahua NVRs, TP-Link Tapo
  • No account, no cloud subscription
